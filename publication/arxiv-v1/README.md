# Publication package: arXiv v1 candidate

This branch stages the formal preprint and review process for:

> Parker Rowen, *Opposition as Local Structure in Open English WordNet: A Corpus-Scale Test of Linguistical Symmetry Theory*.

The current candidate is a 13-page LaTeX manuscript with five figures and four tables. Its structure is deliberately symmetrical around the Results section, but the argument remains fully recoverable in ordinary linear reading order. Visual form demonstrates the representational procedure; it is not treated as evidence for the hypothesis.

## Review state

The manuscript has passed local PDF compilation, text extraction, visual rendering of all thirteen pages, and PDF preflight. Numerical claims are generated from the verified OEWN 2025 corpus artifacts. The paper is being held on a review branch until an external WordNet and lexical-semantics sanity check is complete.

## Intended review scope

The requested external review is bounded to:

- the WordNet data model and use of sense-level relations;
- operational definitions and denominators;
- interpretation of explicit antonym coverage;
- separation of source properties from linguistic conclusions;
- any important neighboring work or methodological omission.

It is not a request for approval of the broader philosophy project, full line editing, coauthorship, or arXiv endorsement.

## Current result

The verified graph contains 135,969 lexical entries, 185,129 senses, 107,519 synsets, 355,064 typed relation records, and 3,994 unique direct antonym pairs. Direct antonymy touches 5.20% of distinct OEWN lemmas. The antonym graph divides into 3,838 components and has a maximum component size of four endpoints. Among the 1,040 antonym pairs with multi-lemma synonym neighborhoods at both poles, 584 have equal neighborhood sizes and the mean size-balance ratio is 0.821.

The corrected claim is that explicit opposition forms recurring local semantic axes with permitted lexical asymmetry in the selected source, not a global mirrored backbone of English.
