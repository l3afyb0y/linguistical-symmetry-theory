# Preprint review and arXiv checklist

## Before contacting Professor Pedersen

- Attach the formal preprint PDF.
- Link the public repository.
- Ask for the bounded methods check described in `ted_pedersen_email.md`.
- Do not request endorsement in the first email.
- Keep the request focused on WordNet use, operational definitions, denominators, and interpretation.

## After technical feedback

- Record every substantive comment in a review log.
- Distinguish factual or methodological corrections from optional presentation preferences.
- Revise the manuscript, code, or both when the mechanism changes.
- Regenerate all measured values and figures from the pipeline after any analytical change.
- Re-run PDF compilation, artifact verification, and visual inspection.
- Add an acknowledgment only with the reviewer's permission.

## arXiv preparation

- Recommended primary category: `cs.CL` (Computation and Language).
- Upload the LaTeX source package, not the locally compiled PDF alone.
- Confirm that `main.tex` is detected as the top-level file and that PDFLaTeX is selected.
- Preview every page in arXiv's generated PDF.
- Copy the title and abstract from `ARXIV_METADATA.md` and check the metadata separately from the PDF.
- Select a distribution license only after reviewing its permanence and compatibility with later journal submission.
- If endorsement is required, begin the submission to obtain arXiv's endorsement-request link, then contact one eligible endorser personally.
- Do not make the final submission until the technical review and any resulting revisions are complete.

## Final integrity checks

- No unresolved citations, references, or placeholders.
- No clipped text, overlapping figures, broken glyphs, or missing figure files.
- All numerical claims match the verified metrics and reproducible scripts.
- The contribution and AI-assistance disclosure remains accurate.
- The public repository identifies the exact version or commit reported by the paper.
