# Email draft to Professor Ted Pedersen

**Subject:** Preprint sanity check: opposition structure in Open English WordNet

Dear Professor Pedersen,

My name is Parker Rowen. I am an independent philosopher and Saint Paul native preparing a preprint titled *Opposition as Local Structure in Open English WordNet: A Corpus-Scale Test of Linguistical Symmetry Theory*.

The project began as a philosophy-of-language question rather than a computational one. I was tracing recurring oppositions across families such as *inward/outward*, *inside/outside*, *internal/external*, *include/exclude*, and *enter/exit*, and asking whether a polarity could persist while changing between direction, location, state, membership, and process. I first explored the idea through hand-drawn semantic maps, informal mathematical reasoning, and whiteboard topology. I then used computational assistance to turn that philosophical observation into a reproducible test over Open English WordNet 2025.

The resulting pipeline preserves words, senses, synsets, and typed relations in one provenance-aware graph, from which it can generate both an alphabetical lexical index and an opposition-first conceptual view. The paper reports 3,994 unique direct antonym pairs. Direct antonymy touches 5.20% of distinct OEWN lemmas, while 71.52% have a co-synonym in at least one sense. The antonym graph is overwhelmingly local rather than globally connected, but 1,040 antonym pairs have multi-lemma synonym neighborhoods at both poles. The experiment therefore revised the original idea rather than simply confirming it: opposition appears to form local semantic axes with permitted lexical asymmetry, not a single mirrored structure across English.

My primary background is independent philosophy, not computational linguistics. Before I submit the paper to arXiv, I would value a bounded technical sanity check from someone familiar with WordNet and lexical semantics. Would you be willing to skim the attached preprint and flag any serious problems in its data model, operational definitions, denominators, use of WordNet relations, or interpretation of the measurements? I am not asking for a full peer review or line edit, and I am not presently asking for arXiv endorsement.

I reached out to you because your work sits directly in the lexical-semantics and WordNet territory this paper enters, and because I hoped to begin close to home. There is something fitting about asking a Minnesota researcher to pressure-test a homegrown philosophy-of-language project before it travels farther.

The code, methodology, and reproducible artifacts are available here:

https://github.com/l3afyb0y/linguistical-symmetry-theory

For transparency, OpenAI ChatGPT assisted with computational formalization, implementation, data analysis, literature discovery, figure generation, and manuscript drafting. I am the sole author and take responsibility for every claim, source, and result.

Thank you for considering it. I understand completely if your schedule does not permit a review.
